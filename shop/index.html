Wildwood Press Shop

A print shop in the wildwoods.

Wildwood Press Shop is a small print studio based in New Hampshire. We design soft, warm, slightly funny apparel and gifts — most of them for people who read past dark, the rest for the kind of people who think "up north" is a personality trait.

Every design starts as a one-line sketch on the back of a receipt or a margin note in a book. Every order is printed and shipped by our production partner (a third-party printer called Printful, who does beautiful work); we just write the words and pick the colors.

The shop opens this summer on Etsy. Subscribe and we'll let you know when the first designs drop.

— Mike, Wildwood Press Shop
