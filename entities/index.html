Entities & Governance

Governance Structure

Bacotti Inc. serves as the central coordination layer for the family's business interests. Our governance framework is designed to provide disciplined oversight, clear accountability, and the structured management necessary to sustain a complex, multi-entity enterprise across generations.

Entity Oversight

We maintain rigorous standards for entity management, including compliance monitoring, reporting cycles, and regular reviews of each holding's strategic alignment with the family's broader objectives. Every entity operates within a defined structure with appropriate checks and balances.

Decision-Making Framework

Our governance approach emphasizes prudent deliberation, risk assessment, and the alignment of actions with long-term family interests. We document decisions, track outcomes, and continuously refine our processes to reflect lessons learned and evolving circumstances.

Transparency & Accountability

We maintain thorough records and clear lines of responsibility. Stakeholders have access to appropriate information flows, and the family is kept informed of material developments affecting the enterprise.
