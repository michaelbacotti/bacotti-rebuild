That characteristic — a high-conviction, low-volume breakout — is the hallmark of a structural re-rating rather than a tactical squeeze. The XLK +1.89%, QQQ +1.85%, XLC +2.86% trifecta was the cleanest mega-cap tech continuation since the June FOMC, and the post-AMZN/MSFT structural bid (AI capex durability per Q2 earnings validation) extended into Tuesday's after-hours with XLK +1.55% and QQQ +0.74%.

The macro framework is now: **soft-landing with disinflation trend intact, 10Y yield range-bound at 4.70-4.80%, VIX compressing toward the 15-handle, mega-cap tech dominance confirmed by three bars of Q2 earnings (TSMC capex raise, MSFT +15.5% surge, AMZN AWS +37% YoY), and Iran de-escalation extracting the geopolitical oil premium.** Friday's Jobs Report is the single biggest test of this framework in the next two weeks. The structural context matters: the US economy has now absorbed three rate-cycle pressure tests (10Y climb from 4.49% to 4.74% in the week ended July 31, soft June PCE all-clear at -0.1% MoM headline, Iran de-escalation over the weekend) without breaking the equity uptrend. **The VIX at 15.61 (vs 1-year mean ~18) is the cleanest single signal that the options market is comfortable with the current regime** — it's the lowest two-day stretch since early July, and the term structure (front-month VIX vs 3-month VIX) is in shallow backwardation consistent with constructive risk-taking, not defensive hedging.
