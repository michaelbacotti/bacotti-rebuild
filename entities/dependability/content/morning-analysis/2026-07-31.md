## The Structural Read

The AMZN +12% / AAPL −7% premarket divergence is the cleanest expression of the current tape regime: the market is sorting AI-related businesses into three buckets — **(1) AI-spend-to-profit translators** (MSFT, AMZN, GOOGL — rewarded on confirmation); **(2) AI-spend-as-headwind** (META — punished on capex concerns); **(3) AI-adjacent but supply-constrained** (AAPL — punished on guidance despite no reported miss). The 10Y at 4.663% suggests the bond market is comfortable with both readings — not pricing recession, not pricing reacceleration.
