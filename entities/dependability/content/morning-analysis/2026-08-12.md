A confirmed Hormuz deal would compress WTI toward $75 and weigh on XLE; absent a deal-clarification catalyst, the risk premium stays.**

- **The 8:30 AM ET CPI print — the binary event of the week.** The consensus +0.2% core MoM / +2.5% YoY would mark the smallest annual core print since February 2026 and confirm the post-June disinflation trajectory. The market is positioned for this: futures bid (+0.24% ES, +0.65% NQ), bond rally (10Y -2bp to 4.68%), VIX 15.28 — all signal a soft-print lean. **The test: does the print come in at or below consensus, or does the core MoM surprise to the upside?** A clean in-line print opens the path to 7,800 (1-month target, +0.93% from current) and supports the September Fed cut thesis.
