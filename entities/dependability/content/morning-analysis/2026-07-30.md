## Overnight Headlines

**Overnight Bounce Recovers Wednesday's Post-FOMC Selloff — ES +1.09%, NQ +1.05%, RTY +0.79%:** Markets are stabilizing after Wednesday's -1.52% SPX selloff, the steepest single-day drop since the late-June Iran escalation. The 30Y held at 5.193% Wednesday and the 10Y at 4.62%, signaling that the bond market has found a clearing level after the steepener.

**Oil Pulls Back to $83.52 (-1.11%) as Iran Premium Cools; Gold Surges +2.50% Overnight:** WTI crude settled at $83.52 in early European trade, down from Wednesday's $84.46 settle (-1.11%) — modest cooling of the Iran premium that pushed WTI from Tuesday's $79.26 close to Wednesday's $84.46 area. The [Strait of Hormuz remains effectively closed](https://straits.live) (Day 152; ~10 ships last week vs ~88/day normal), but the market is pricing de-escalation rhetoric (Trump diplomatic engagement via China, Pakistan) more than the active threat. Gold is the standout overnight mover: GC=F at $4,135.60, up **+2.50%** from Wednesday's $4,034.70 settle — the largest single-session gold move in weeks and a clear risk-off bid. GLD prints at $373.95; the gold surge alongside a flat dollar (DXY 100.75, -0.05%) suggests genuine safe-haven demand.

**VIX at 20.66 Confirms Hawkish-Dissent Vol Regime:** The +13.5% single-session VIX spike from Tuesday's 18.21 close is the cleanest post-FOMC vol regime the market has printed under the Warsh Fed: not panic-level, not benign, but a "hawkish-dissent holding pattern" that keeps bid/ask spreads wide and protective put pricing elevated. A 10% OTM put on SPY (the ~$656 strike for 90-day expiry, SPY $729.46, implied SPX ~7,295) now costs roughly 1.20-1.30% of notional — up from 1.05-1.10% pre-FOMC but below the 1-year mean of ~1.40%. AMZN Q2 after-close is the next binary vol catalyst.

**AMZN Q2 Anchors the Day — Consensus EPS $1.82 / $196.18B Revenue / AWS Growth the Read:** Amazon reports Q2 after the close (consensus EPS $1.82 on $196.18B revenue, +17.0% YoY; EPS range $1.56-$2.10; 46 analysts per AlphaStreet). The structural reads: AWS growth (~+18-28% YoY per [TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/investing/amazon-aws-q2-2026-earnings-preview) raised estimates) and capex guidance ($200B 2026 baseline; rumored raise would re-anchor AI infrastructure durability). After Alphabet raised 2026 capex to $195-205B last week, AMZN's guidance is the test for whether the AI capex cycle is broadening or narrowing.

The interaction between Wednesday's hawkish-dissent FOMC and Thursday's overnight bounce sets up a structurally interesting tape. The bond market absorbed the 30Y +9 bp spike to 5.193% and the 10Y held at 4.62%, suggesting the Warsh Fed's 9-3 hold was fully priced by yesterday's close.
