About Bacotti Inc.

Who We Are

Bacotti Inc. is a private family office and stewardship company. We are the institutional backbone of the Bacotti family's business interests — a coordination layer that provides governance, administration, financial oversight, and strategic guidance across a portfolio of entities and investments.

Our Role

We exist to ensure that the family's enterprise is managed with the discipline, continuity, and long-horizon thinking that institutional capital requires. We are not an investment fund, not a bank, and not a consulting firm. We are a permanent part of the family's institutional infrastructure.

Our Approach

We are deliberate, rigorous, and conservative. We move slowly when speed would compromise judgment. We document thoroughly. We plan for contingencies. We maintain the infrastructure that allows the family to think clearly about its future — because clear thinking requires solid ground.

Standards

Bacotti Inc. holds itself to institutional standards of governance, reporting, and accountability. We maintain proper financial records, comply with all applicable regulations, and ensure that every entity under our management is in good standing and operating within its defined mandate.

Portfolio of Entities

Under our stewardship are a portfolio of operating entities, holding companies, and special-purpose vehicles. Each entity has a defined mandate, a separate governance structure, and a clear relationship to the family's overall objectives. We coordinate across these entities to ensure consistency of governance, alignment of incentives, and proper segregation of risk. We do not intermingle funds or operations across entities in ways that would compromise clarity or expose the family to unintended liability.

Governance

Our governance model follows the same principles applied to a well-run institution: clear roles and responsibilities, documented decision-making, regular board activity, and separation of duties. Each operating entity has its own governance documents — operating agreements, bylaws, board resolutions — that define its scope and constraints. We update these documents when the law changes, when the entity's circumstances change, or when our family principals direct a revision. We do not cut corners on governance because governance is the foundation that allows everything else to function.

Financial Oversight

We maintain proper financial records for every entity under our stewardship. Each entity has its own books, its own accounting cadence, and its own reporting to relevant authorities (federal, state, and local). Where independent audits are required by the entity's structure or by the family's standards, we engage qualified auditors and act on their findings. We do not commingle funds across entities in ways that would obscure the financial position of any one entity.

Strategic Guidance

We help the family think clearly about its future. This is not the same as "investment advice" — we are not registered investment advisers and do not provide personalized investment recommendations. Instead, we help frame the family's overall strategy: what entities to maintain, what to wind down, what new directions to consider, what risks to mitigate. We bring institutional thinking to bear on the family's decisions, but the family principals make the final calls.

Long-Horizon Orientation

Family stewardship is measured in decades, not quarters. Our orientation is permanent — we are not building an entity to be sold or to deliver a return to outside investors. We plan for multi-generational continuity: what works for the family today should still be coherent for the family in 20, 50, and 100 years. This means resisting short-term pressures in favor of long-term soundness, and being willing to make slow, deliberate decisions when speed would compromise judgment.

Discretion and Confidentiality

Family matters are private matters. We treat the family's information with the discretion that institutional stewardship requires. We do not disclose entity structures, financial positions, or strategic plans outside the family and its designated advisors. We are careful about what we publish publicly about the family's enterprise — see our public disclosures for the entities that have chosen to make their existence known.

What We Are Not

We are not an investment fund. We do not accept outside capital and do not manage money for non-family clients. We are not a bank — we do not take deposits or make loans. We are not a law firm or an accounting firm — we engage outside professionals for specialized work. We are not a consulting firm — we do not sell our time or our advice to third parties. We are a private stewardship company, and our only client is the family.

How We Engage Outside Help

For specialized work — legal advice, tax planning, audit, custody, insurance, real estate, and the like — we engage qualified outside professionals. We maintain relationships with firms we trust, and we review those relationships periodically. We do not accept referrals from people we have not independently vetted, and we do not pay referral fees. When we engage an outside firm, we expect professional standards comparable to our own: documented work, clear communications, no surprises.

Launch and History

Bacotti Inc. was launched in 2024 as a formal vehicle for family stewardship. Prior to incorporation, the family's enterprise was managed informally across multiple operating entities and personal holdings. The 2024 incorporation created the permanent institutional layer that allows the family to coordinate across entities with consistent governance. We are a Delaware C-corporation, though we operate with the governance discipline of a multi-generational institution rather than the operating tempo of a typical small business. Since 2024, the entity has held continuous good standing in Delaware and in every state where it has filed to do business.

Editorial Standards and Process

For the public-facing entities under our stewardship, we maintain editorial standards consistent with independent publishing: every piece is reviewed by at least one other person before publication, every factual claim is checked against a primary source, and every correction is dated and posted prominently. Our editorial process follows the principles documented on the SpaceOrbitals about page: original reporting, verification, hedging where uncertainty exists, and editorial independence. We do not accept payment for coverage or sponsored content across any of our entities.

Credentials of Leadership

Leadership at Bacotti Inc. holds professional credentials relevant to family stewardship: the principal is a licensed attorney with active bar admission, has earned the CFA charter (Chartered Financial Analyst) for investment analysis work, and has experience in trust administration and estate planning. Several decades of combined experience in finance, law, and operations inform the governance approach. The principal also serves as a fiduciary on family trust matters.

Corrections Policy

For our public-facing entities, our corrections policy is documented on each entity's about page: if we discover an error or a reader flags one with a source link, we post a dated correction note at the top of the affected piece within 48 hours. We do not silently fix errors; every substantive correction is visible. For internal entity matters, corrections follow the documentation protocols of the relevant entity, including dated entries in the governance log and notification to the relevant counterparty where applicable.
