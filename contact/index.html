Contact Bacotti Inc.

Stewardship Email

For legitimate matters related to family stewardship, governance, or administration of entities under our management, reach us at: stewardship@bacotti.com. We check this mailbox regularly and respond within 2 business days to legitimate inquiries.

What We Do Not Accept

Bacotti Inc. is a private family office. We do not accept unsolicited inquiries, solicitations, cold outreach, partnership pitches, vendor proposals, sales calls, investment opportunities, business propositions, or pitches of any kind that are not initiated by us or by a known counterparty. Email pitches to the stewardship address will be filtered and not opened.

Press Inquiries

We do not engage with press inquiries about the family's private matters. For inquiries related to our public-facing entities (SpaceOrbitals, Bithues, Dependability Holdings, Succession Holding LLC, Triadive, Tredey), contact those entities directly — they have their own press contacts. We do not coordinate press for the family's private matters or comment on family enterprise, ownership, or strategy.

Job Inquiries

We do not accept unsolicited resumes or job applications. Bacotti Inc. is a small organization and we engage new staff through targeted searches, referrals from trusted advisors, or direct outreach to candidates we have already identified. If you are interested in working with one of our public-facing entities, contact that entity directly.

Service Providers and Vendors

We engage service providers through established relationships and do not accept unsolicited vendor pitches. If you are a current service provider working with Bacotti Inc. or one of our entities, you can reach your usual contact at the stewardship address. New vendors are engaged through formal procurement processes and rarely on the basis of unsolicited outreach.

Existing Counterparties

If you are an existing counterparty (bank, custodian, attorney, accountant, advisor, board member, or counterparty to any entity under our stewardship) and have a question related to your engagement, please contact your usual Bacotti Inc. representative or write to stewardship@bacotti.com with the relevant context. We will route your message appropriately.

Privacy and Discretion

Any information you send to stewardship@bacotti.com is handled with discretion consistent with our stewardship role. We do not share correspondence with third parties except as required by law or as necessary to fulfill a legitimate request you have made. We retain correspondence consistent with our document retention policy and applicable recordkeeping requirements.

Response Times

We aim to respond to legitimate inquiries within 2 business days. Complex matters may take longer; if your matter requires engagement with outside counsel, custodians, or other third parties, we will set expectations on timing once we have routed the request appropriately.

Mailing Address

For physical mail, legal correspondence, or document delivery, contact stewardship@bacotti.com to request the current mailing address. We do not publish a physical address on this site; we provide it on a need-to-know basis to verified counterparties.

Verification

If you need to verify the existence or good standing of any entity under our stewardship, contact us at stewardship@bacotti.com with the specific request. We do not provide verification of entities to parties who do not have a legitimate need, and we will not confirm or deny entity ownership structures to outside parties without prior family approval.

Our Launch and History

Bacotti Inc. was launched in 2024 as a formal vehicle for family stewardship. The 2024 incorporation created the permanent institutional layer that allows us to coordinate across multiple entities with consistent governance. Prior to incorporation, the family's enterprise was managed informally; the formal entity now provides the governance discipline appropriate to multi-generational stewardship.

Editorial Standards and Process

For the public-facing entities under our stewardship, we maintain editorial standards consistent with independent publishing: every piece is reviewed by at least one other person before publication, every factual claim is checked against a primary source, and every correction is dated and posted prominently. Our editorial process follows the principles documented on each entity's about page: original reporting, verification, hedging where uncertainty exists, and editorial independence.

Credentials of Leadership

Leadership at Bacotti Inc. holds professional credentials relevant to family stewardship: the principal is a licensed attorney with active bar admission, has earned the CFA charter (Chartered Financial Analyst) for investment analysis work, and has experience in trust administration and estate planning. The team has combined decades of experience in finance, law, and operations.

Corrections Policy

For our public-facing entities, our corrections policy is: if we discover an error or a reader flags one with a source link, we post a dated correction note at the top of the affected piece within 48 hours. We do not silently fix errors; every substantive correction is visible. For internal entity matters, corrections follow the documentation protocols of the relevant entity.

Office Hours

We are reachable by email during US business hours (9am-6pm Eastern, Monday-Friday). Emails received outside those hours are processed the following business day. We do not maintain 24/7 phone coverage; for urgent matters involving time-sensitive decisions or ongoing transactions, reach out by email with "URGENT" in the subject line and we will prioritize.

Languages

Our team operates primarily in English. For matters in other languages, please engage with a translator first or send correspondence in English; we will reply in English. If you need translation services for a specific engagement, we can recommend external translators on request.
